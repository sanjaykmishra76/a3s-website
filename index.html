Questions

The things drivers ask us first.

How much is the deposit, and do I get it back?

The deposit is $150 and it is refundable. We return it after you hand the car back, once it has been checked for damage and any outstanding tolls or tickets have cleared.

What do I need to bring when I collect the car?

Your Texas driver's licence, your Uber driver approval, and the $150 deposit. Bring those and you can sign and drive the same day.

Is there a mileage cap?

No cap on rideshare miles. We ask that personal use stays reasonable — the car is a working vehicle, not a weekend road-trip rental.

What happens if the car breaks down?

Call us. Roadside assistance is included 24/7, and if the car needs to go in for more than a day we put you in a replacement so you are not losing earnings.

How do I pay, and how do I know what I owe?

Payment is weekly. Every week you receive a statement by email showing what you earned, what was deducted and what balance is carried forward — so there is never a question about where the number came from.

What if I want to stop renting?

Give us notice, return the car in good condition and we close out your account. There is no long-term contract and no early-termination penalty.
